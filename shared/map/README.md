# The map

The world is a fictional island that riffs on the Bay Area, on a 456 × 504
tile grid. `x` grows east and `y` grows south. Fog City (San Francisco) is the
biggest region: its west side is squeezed into a compact beach town, the rest
is drawn to scale, and everything farther than 7.5 km from its center is
squeezed toward it, so Redwood Reach, Golden Hills, and Silicon Vale shrink
around it. `regionNames` in `places.ts` holds the regions' display names.
Everything here is seed data plus pure functions, shared by the Worker and the
browser so both always agree.

- **`grid.ts`**: the grid's size, its ground kinds, and `warp(lon, lat)`, which
  maps real coordinates onto the grid. It uses only arithmetic and `Math.sqrt`,
  which every JavaScript engine computes identically.
- **`geography.json`**: generated by `pnpm map` (`scripts/geography.ts`) from
  U.S. Census TIGER data: coastline and lakes, parks, campuses, San Francisco's
  major streets, main streets and freeways elsewhere, and rail lines, all cut
  to a hand-drawn island outline with a smoothed coast. The ground is stored as
  run lengths per row and the roads as polylines.
- **`terrain.ts`**: decodes the ground grid.
- **`route.ts`**: rasterizes the roads, defines rides (Bay Rail, Peninsula Rail,
  and ferries, each linking two stations), and `route(from, to)`. A route walks
  to the nearest road tile reachable from the Ferry Building, takes the quickest
  path over roads and rides, and walks to the destination. Every ride adds a
  fixed boarding time. The search is deterministic, with ties broken by tile
  index, so the server's arrival time and the browser's animation match.
- **`places.ts`**: every place, with its region, neighborhood, interest tags,
  activity, real location, and capacity. A place's spots grow outward from its
  location over free, dry ground off the roads, keeping a one-tile gap from
  other places. Agents stand on spots, and a unique index on (place, tile) in
  the database enforces capacity.

To add a place, add a row with its real latitude and longitude. `map.test.ts`
checks that spots are on dry ground, off the roads, and not shared, and that
every place is reachable. Moving the map or a place changes spot tiles, so
people already standing on old tiles must be moved to their place's new spots.
